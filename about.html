Who we are

Growing in faith, serving with love.

Our mission

To share the hope of Jesus Christ and make disciples across Maryland.

We are a Christ-centered church family committed to worship, discipleship, and community care. Our mission is rooted in the love of God and the teachings of Jesus, and we seek to welcome everyone with grace, humility, and hope.

From weekly worship to local outreach and global mission, we believe the church should be a place where people find belonging, spiritual growth, and practical encouragement.

Our values

Worship

We gather to praise God, study His Word, and experience His presence together.

Community

We build genuine relationships, care for one another, and celebrate life together.

Service

We serve our neighbors with compassion through outreach, charity, and practical care.

Mission

We share the gospel and participate in ministry both locally and around the world.
