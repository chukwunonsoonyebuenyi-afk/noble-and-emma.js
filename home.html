Who we are

Grounded in the Bible. Focused on the mission.

We are a vibrant Christian community rooted in the love of Jesus and the Seventh-day Adventist tradition. Through prayer, biblical teaching, and service, we help people grow in faith and live out the mission of sharing God's love with the world.
